🔑 Key Takeaways from These Stories

  • Mia's approach saves the most money – Buying used and keeping the vehicle long-term minimizes depreciation and allows for payment-free years.
  • James's approach costs 3x more – Perpetual leasing means always having a payment and never building equity.
  • Taylor's approach is a compromise – More expensive than Mia's but allows for newer vehicles with some equity building.
  • Payment-free years are powerful – The years without a car payment let you save or invest that money elsewhere.
  • Your choice depends on your priorities – Low cost? Always new? Balance? Each approach serves different goals.

Buy vs. Lease Comparison

Understanding Your Auto Financing Options

🏠

BUYING

Building Ownership

📊 Monthly Payment
Higher initially, but builds equity with each payment
✅ Ownership Status
You own the vehicle. It's yours to keep, sell, or modify.
🛣️ Mileage Limits
UNLIMITED - Drive as much as you need
🔧 Condition Requirements
No restrictions - maintain as you choose
🎯 End Result
You own an asset worth $12,000+
After 6 Years: YOU OWN AN ASSET WORTH $12,000
📅

LEASING

Temporary Use

📊 Monthly Payment
Lower monthly payment, but no equity built
❌ Ownership Status
You never own it. Must return at lease end.
🛣️ Mileage Limits
LIMITED - Typically 10k-15k miles/year. Penalties for overages ($0.25/mile)
🔧 Condition Requirements
Must return in "good condition" or pay fees for excess wear
🎯 End Result
Return the car with nothing to show
After 6 Years (2 leases): YOU PAID $30,000, OWN NOTHING

6-Year Total Cost Comparison

Buying
Net Cost: $18,000
(You own $12K asset)
Leasing
Net Cost: $30,000
(You own nothing)

💡 Key Financial Insights

Buy New Outcome:
After loan payoff, you can drive payment-free for years while the car still has value
Lease Outcome:
Perpetual payments - never own anything, always making payments
Long-Term Winner:
Buying saves $12,000+ over 6 years and leaves you with an asset
Best for Leasing:
Business use (tax write-off) or if you drive very few miles and want newest vehicle
